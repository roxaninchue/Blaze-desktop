# Ad lab

Five genuinely different ads, scored honestly, best first.

## 1. Brief
- Product or offer (a listing from product-listing, a URL or a description), the audience (who, where, what they care about), platform and placement (Feed, Reels/TikTok, Stories, Google Search), budget level, goal (sales, leads, messages on WhatsApp) and any offer or deadline.
- Brand voice from the brand kit / memory. Market and currency (e.g. Zambia, ZMW) when it matters.

## 2. Write 5 variants, each a different angle
1. Problem → solution
2. Benefit / transformation (before → after)
3. Social proof (a real review the seller has; never invent one, else use "Customers tell us…" only if true)
4. Offer / urgency (only real deadlines and stock)
5. Curiosity or pattern-break hook (question, bold claim the product can back up)
Each variant: hook (first 3 seconds / first line, ≤ 8 words), primary text (≤ 125 chars for Feed, ≤ 90 for Stories), headline (≤ 40 chars), CTA button, visual or video idea (first frame, shots, captions on screen). For Google Search: 3 headlines ≤ 30 chars and 2 descriptions ≤ 90 chars.

## 3. Score (0-10 each, be strict)
- **Hook**: would it stop a scroll in 1-3 s for this audience? Specific beats clever.
- **Clarity**: can someone tell what it is, who it's for and why in one read? No jargon.
- **CTA**: one clear action, matches the goal and the button, low friction.
- Total out of 30. Also flag policy risks (personal attributes "Are you lonely?", before/after health claims, misleading prices) and fix them.

## 4. Return
- A table sorted by total, best first: rank · angle · hook · H · C · CTA · total.
- Then each variant in full, with one line on why it scored as it did and one improvement.
- Test plan: run the top 2-3 against each other with the same audience and budget for 3-4 days, judge on cost per result (not likes), keep the winner, rewrite the loser's hook.
- Offer: visuals in the Design studio with the brand kit (post 1080×1350 or story 1080×1920), or a content-calendar slot.

## Checklist
- [ ] Brief has product, audience, platform, goal.
- [ ] 5 variants with 5 different angles; lengths within platform limits.
- [ ] Scores strict and explained; policy risks fixed.
- [ ] Sorted best first with a test plan.
- [ ] No invented reviews, deadlines or claims.
